**We do not want your data. We do not collect your data.**

- **No accounts.** This library runs in static mode: there is no
  registration, no login, and no way for us to know who you are.
- **No tracking.** No analytics, no fingerprinting, no advertising, no
  third-party scripts or cookies of any kind.
- **No personal data storage.** This deployment has no database. There is
  nothing to breach and nothing to sell — by design, not by promise.
- **Your progress stays with you.** Course progress is stored in your own
  browser (localStorage) and never leaves your device. Clearing your
  browser data erases it.
- **Functional preferences only.** If you switch the theme or language, that
  preference is kept in a small cookie in your browser purely so the site
  remembers your choice. It identifies nothing and nobody, and is never
  transmitted anywhere for any other purpose.

is not a product or a service, and no commercial activity — direct or
indirect — takes place here.
